Research Projects & Publications

Data science and modelling for the outbreak risk of climate-sensitive infectious diseases

On this page

  • Featured first-author studies
  • Peer-reviewed publications
  • PhD thesis
  • Manuscripts under review & in preparation
  • Selected code repositories

My research applies data science, machine learning, and mechanistic modelling to predict the outbreak risk of infectious diseases, focusing on how climatic, ecological, socioeconomic, and mobility factors shape disease emergence and spread. Much of my recent work concerns the climate-driven expansion of vector-borne diseases across Europe — West Nile virus, dengue, and chikungunya — and the methods needed to forecast and monitor them.
